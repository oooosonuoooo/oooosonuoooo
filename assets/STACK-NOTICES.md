The graphics use locally embedded SVG artwork. Product marks identify the tools shown and are subject to their owners' trademark policies.

Most monochrome logomarks come from [Simple Icons 16.36.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.36.0/icons), and the CSS3 shield comes from [Simple Icons 13.21.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/13.21.0/icons/css3.svg). The project publishes its contributions under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.36.0/LICENSE.md) and documents [brand-specific terms](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.36.0/DISCLAIMER.md). Marks are sized for the tiles, and some monochrome fills change with the theme.
